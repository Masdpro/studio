# **App Name**: Swiftbuy

## Core Features:

- Vendor Registration: Allow vendors to register and manage their profiles, including business information and contact details.
- Product Listing: Enable vendors to upload products with descriptions, prices, and images.
- Customer Order Placement: Allow customers to browse products, place orders, and manage their shopping carts.
- Escrow Service: Integrate an escrow service for secure payment processing, ensuring that deliverers get paid upon successful delivery confirmation.
- Route Optimization Tool: Suggest optimal delivery routes to deliverers based on real-time traffic data, delivery locations, and time windows, to improve delivery efficiency and reduce travel time.

## Style Guidelines:

- A vibrant coral (#FF8A65) to convey energy and freshness, aligning with the dynamic nature of on-demand food delivery.
- Light beige (#F5F5DC), a subtle variation of the coral hue, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.
- A mellow yellow (#F0E68C), analogous to coral, used to highlight key interactive elements and call-to-action buttons, creating visual contrast.
- Clean, modern fonts to ensure readability on various screen sizes.
- Use clear and intuitive icons to represent various app features and food categories.
- Simple, grid-based layout for easy navigation and a visually appealing product display.
